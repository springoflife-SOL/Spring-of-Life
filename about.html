About us

Who is Spring of Life?

A non-denominational Bible institution that has its roots in missionary work from Germany.

Our roots

It started in Germany

Spring of Life is not tied to any single denomination. What holds us together is the Word itself — and the conviction that anyone can read it, question it, and find something true in it.

Global expansion

From one place, outward

From its roots in Germany, SL has continued to expand its work internationally, reaching youth and communities across the world.

From Germany
  • ToAfrica
  • ToSouth America
  • ToAsia

In Korea

Since 2014

  1. On campus

    SL started on campuses in Korea to encourage Bible reading among young people.

  2. Alongside churches

    SL has since branched out to cooperate with various international churches in Korea, including New Hope Church and Shining Light Church.

  3. In Hongdae and beyond

    Our gatherings — movie nights, trivia nights, honest conversations — have brought together people from all over the world.

Mission

SL and its volunteers aim to achieve personal growth through various activities by putting the Word into practice in society, their daily life, and around the world.

Vision

SL is a global organization seeking to create positive influence through the Word.

What we stand for

Four things we keep coming back to

Personal Growth

Developing ourselves through the Word and various activities, and applying it in our daily life.

Putting the Word into Practice

Turning the Word into action in society, our community, and around the world.

Creating Global Positive Influence

Inspiring positive change and making a meaningful impact globally through the Word.

Partners

We work alongside international churches in Korea, including New Hope Church and Shining Light Church.
