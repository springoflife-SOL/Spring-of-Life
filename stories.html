Stories

Testimony from our Seonbae (선배)

Four people who came to Korea, met Spring of Life, and left with something they didn’t arrive with.

Lorena

Brazil

I was a non-believer, and I had a lot of doubts about religion and the Bible. But through SL, I started to see that the Bible actually makes sense and that God has a purpose for my life. Now I’m doing many activities together with other believers and helping others find their purpose. Looking back, I feel like coming to Korea happened for a reason. It was a good experience. I miss everyone.

Jenny

Chile

The world for me was depressing and hopeless. There were many times when I wanted to give up, but through SL, I learned that this world was not created according to God’s will, and that we have a role in rebuilding it, starting from my inner self. I also gained the tools and confidence to prepare for mission work back in my country. Now, I want to give back by supporting SL’s work and helping others find the same hope I found. Fighting!

Gracia

Indonesia

I used to be stressed and overwhelmed, often relying on distractions to cope. Through SL and guidance from my mentor, I learned that true peace comes from knowing God through the Bible. It wasn’t an easy journey, but it changed me. Now, we’ve started an organization in Indonesia along with others who share the same heart, and we donate to SL to help more people experience what we did.

Mahdi

Iran

I have lived overseas as a refugee, so I often questioned God and why I had to go through so many hardships. I wasn’t a Christian, but after learning God’s Word through SL, I began to understand why I went through those experiences and realized how blessed I am. Now, I want to give back to SL and support the people in my country, just as SL supported me and helped change my life.
