FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Do I need to be a Christian to join?
No. Several of the people whose stories are on this site described themselves as non-believers when they started, and some had never opened a Bible before.
Which denomination are you?
None. Spring of Life is non-denominational. We cooperate with a range of international churches in Korea, including New Hope Church and Shining Light Church.
How long does it take?
Programs like Finding Inner Peace start with three sessions. After that, how far you go is up to you.
Where did Spring of Life come from?
SL has its roots in missionary work from Germany, and has since expanded to Africa, South America, and Asia. We have been in Korea since 2014.

A non-denominational Bible institution rooted in missionary work from Germany, reaching youth and communities across Africa, South America, and Asia.
