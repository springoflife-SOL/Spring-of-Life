Programs

How we walk with the youth

Five ways in. Pick the one that sounds like you — or tell us where you are and we’ll help you choose.

Bible Deep Dive

Explore the Bible, one on one

Go through the text with someone who has read it closely. Bring your questions — especially the uncomfortable ones.

Faith & You

Discover yourself through faith

Less about doctrine, more about you. What you believe, why you believe it, and what that means for the life you’re actually living.

Word & Reflect

Build a Bible reflection habit

A rhythm you can keep. Short readings, honest reflection, and a group that notices when you go quiet.

Bible Mentoring

Grow through biblical guidance

One mentor, over time. Someone who knows your name, your story, and where you said you wanted to go.

Finding Inner Peace

Discover the roots of true peace

Start here if you’re tired. Three sessions on where peace actually comes from, and why it keeps slipping away.

How to start

Three steps, no pressure

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Tell us what you’re looking for. No background in the Bible is required, and you don’t have to call yourself a believer.

  2. 02

    Meet a mentor

    We match you with a mentor and find a time that fits your week.

  3. 03

    Grow together

    Start with three sessions. After that, it’s up to you how far you want to go.
