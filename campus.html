What it’s about

We In Campus

Spring of Life is moving onto campus. We In Campus brings Bible discussion groups, peer mentoring, and community events to university students — a way to stay rooted in faith without stepping off campus to do it.

What it provides

Four ways to get involved

  • Campus Groups

    Weekly student-led gatherings, hosted on or near your campus.

  • Peer Mentoring

    Paired with an upperclassman or alum who’s walked the same halls.

  • Events & Retreats

    Seasonal retreats, welcome-week meetups, and finals-week check-ins.

  • Leadership Training

    A track for students who want to help lead a group of their own.
